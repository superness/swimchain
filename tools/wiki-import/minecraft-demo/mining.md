# Mining

![Pixel-art pickaxe striking stone]({{media:mining.png}})

**Mining** is the backbone of progression in [[Minecraft]]: nearly every tier of [[Tools and Armor]] starts as ore in a wall. The world runs from the build ceiling down to Y = -64, and what you find depends heavily on how deep you are.

## Depth guide

- **Coal and copper** are abundant near the surface and inside mountains.
- **Iron** appears almost everywhere, with rich veins high in peaks and a broad band through the mid-depths. Iron is the workhorse metal: buckets, anvils, rails, and your first real armor.
- **Gold** concentrates lower down (and riddles the badlands biome). It matters for [[Brewing]] ingredients, powered rails ([[Transportation]]), and bartering in [[The Nether]].
- **Redstone dust** sits near the bottom of the world — fuel for everything on the [[Redstone]] page.
- **Lapis lazuli**, the [[Enchanting]] currency, bands through the middle depths.
- **[[Diamond]]** generates below Y = 16 and gets more common with depth; many miners branch-mine around Y = -59, just above the bedrock floor.
- **Emeralds** generate only under mountains and are far easier to earn from a [[Villager]].

[[The Nether]] has its own geology: quartz, nether gold, and the buried ancient debris that becomes [[Netherite]].

## Techniques

**Branch mining** — a central tunnel with parallel side branches every third block — maximizes ore exposed per block broken and is the safest way to farm diamonds. **Cave mining** is faster but riskier: dripstone and lush cave systems expose enormous ore faces, patrolled by everything on the [[Mobs]] page. Carry a water bucket for lava, and remember ores below the stone layer sit in deepslate, which breaks about twice as slowly.

## Safety rules

Never dig straight down. Light your tunnels or they backfill with monsters. Cache a [[Crafting]] table, furnace, and spare pickaxes at a base camp, bring real food ([[Farming]] pays off underground), and treat the hiss of a [[Creeper]] behind you with the respect it has earned.
