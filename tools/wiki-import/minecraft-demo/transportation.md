# Transportation

An infinite world makes **transportation** a real engineering domain in [[Minecraft]]. Sprinting is where everyone starts; nobody ambitious stays there.

## Animals

**Horses** are the classic upgrade: tame one by riding until it stops bucking, saddle it, and gallop — the fastest land travel short of ice tricks. Donkeys and mules trade speed for chest storage; llamas form pack caravans and spit at wolves. **Pigs** technically steer with a carrot on a stick, at a pace best described as humiliating. In [[The Nether]], the lava-walking **strider** is the only sane way across the seas.

## Boats and rails

**Boats** are free, fast on water, and double as portable [[Mobs]] traps (ask the [[Enderman]]). Rowing a boat on **blue ice** breaks land-speed records — ice highways in the Nether, where every block equals eight Overworld blocks, are endgame infrastructure. **Minecarts** on rails shine for cargo and repeatable routes: powered rails (gold from [[Mining]]) keep carts moving, hopper carts collect items, and a [[Redstone]] station turns a track into a subway.

## Teleport tricks

Thrown ender pearls ([[Enderman]] droppings) teleport you across gaps and out of trouble. Beds and respawn anchors move your *respawn*, which is transportation for pessimists. And Nether portal pairs, placed with a little division by eight, are the backbone of every long-distance network — see [[The Nether]].

## Elytra

The **elytra** from [[The End]]'s cities changes the game's geometry: equip it as a chestplate, jump, and glide. Firework rockets ([[Farming]] supplies the paper via sugar cane) boost you into genuine flight, and a Riptide trident in the rain does it hydraulically. Once you fly, every earlier method becomes freight — which is exactly what rails, ice roads, and mule trains remain for.
