# Minecraft

![Pixel-art grass block]({{media:minecraft-grass-block.png}})

**Minecraft** is a sandbox game about placing blocks and going on adventures in a procedurally generated world. Every world is effectively infinite, carved into cubic blocks you can break, carry, and rebuild. There is no single win condition: some players chase the credits by slaying the [[Ender Dragon]], some build redstone computers, some farm potatoes in peace. This wiki collects practical guides to the game's core systems.

## Where to start

If this is your first world, read [[Getting Started]] — it walks the classic first-day arc: wood, [[Crafting]] table, stone tools, shelter before dark. From there, [[Mining]] takes you underground toward [[Diamond]], and [[Smelting]] turns what you dig up into ingots, glass, and cooked food.

## Core systems

- [[Crafting]] — turning raw materials into tools, blocks, and machines.
- [[Mining]] — ore distribution by depth, cave exploration, and staying alive underground.
- [[Smelting]] — furnaces, blast furnaces, smokers, and what to burn in them.
- [[Farming]] — crops, animal breeding, and never being hungry again.
- [[Tools and Armor]] — the material tiers and what each is actually for.
- [[Combat]] — weapons, attack timing, shields, and not dying.
- [[Redstone]] — the game's wiring, from self-opening doors to full machines.
- [[Brewing]] — potions and status effects.
- [[Enchanting]] — spending experience to make gear dramatically better.

## The world

The Overworld is stitched together from [[Biomes]] — deserts, jungles, oceans, mushroom islands — each with its own resources and dangers. Beneath it all run caves, and beyond it lie two other dimensions: [[The Nether]], a hazardous hellscape holding the ingredients for [[Brewing]] and [[Netherite]], and [[The End]], the floating void islands where the [[Ender Dragon]] waits. Getting around all of this efficiently is its own craft — see [[Transportation]].

## Creatures

The catch-all guide is [[Mobs]]. The famous ones have their own pages: the [[Creeper]], the game's exploding unofficial mascot; the [[Enderman]], a teleporting nightmare with strict eye-contact rules; and the [[Villager]], whose trades can carry an entire late game. The two boss monsters — the [[Ender Dragon]] and the player-summoned [[Wither]] — get pages of their own.

## Progression at a glance

Wood → stone → iron → [[Diamond]] → [[Netherite]], with [[Enchanting]] and [[Brewing]] multiplying everything along the way. The path runs through all three dimensions, and none of it is mandatory. It is a sandbox: the credits are optional, the cathedral is not.
