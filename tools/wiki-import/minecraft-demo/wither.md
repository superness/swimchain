# Wither

The **Wither** is the boss you build yourself: three skulls on a T of soul sand, assembled by hand, regretted immediately. Where the [[Ender Dragon]] waits politely in its own dimension, the Wither is summoned wherever you were foolish enough to place the last skull — and it starts the fight by exploding.

## The recipe

Four blocks of **soul sand** (or soul soil) from [[The Nether]] in a T shape, crowned with three **wither skeleton skulls**. The skulls are the hard part: wither skeletons stalk nether fortresses and drop their heads rarely, so farming three usually means many fortress laps or a Looting sword from [[Enchanting]]. Place the final skull last — the moment the shape completes, the boss flashes, grows, and detonates its spawn blast.

## What you're in for

The Wither is a three-headed floating skull volley-firing explosive projectiles that leave the **Wither effect** — a black-hearted drain that, unlike poison, can kill you outright. It shrugs off [[Mobs]] and players alike, smashes through terrain to reach you (there is no hiding in a bunker; it breaks the bunker), and at half health armors itself against arrows, forcing melee.

## Winning

Preparation is the whole fight: top-tier [[Tools and Armor]], Smite on the sword (the Wither is undead), golden apples, and [[Brewing]] output — strength, regeneration, and health splashes. Classic strategies: summon it under bedrock at the top of [[The Nether]] so it wedges itself while you flail at its feet, or fight in open desert where its explosions cost nothing you care about. Do not summon it at home. Everyone does it once.

## The prize

It drops the **nether star**, the sole ingredient for the **beacon** — a pyramid-powered monument granting area effects like haste and speed to everything around your base. A haste beacon over a [[Mining]] site or [[Netherite]] dig is the closest the game comes to industrial machinery, which is why players keep building this thing on purpose.
