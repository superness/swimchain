# Enderman

The **Enderman** is [[Minecraft]]'s tall, black, teleporting enigma — three blocks of silhouette with glowing purple eyes, found rarely in the Overworld, occasionally in [[The Nether]]'s warped forests, and everywhere in [[The End]], its home dimension.

## The eye-contact rule

Endermen are neutral [[Mobs]] with one hair trigger: put your crosshair on their eyes or torso, even from dozens of blocks away, and they open their jaw, shake, and hunt you. They also anger if you attack them. Either way, an angry enderman teleports behind you and hits hard — and keeps teleporting through the fight, shrugging off arrows entirely (they warp away from projectiles, which makes the [[Combat]] bow useless here).

## Fighting one anyway

Their weaknesses are environmental. Endermen take damage from water — rain, a river, or a splash bottle all hurt them — and they are three blocks tall, so standing under a two-block ceiling means they cannot reach you while you swing freely. A boat is a classic trap: an enderman that wanders in cannot teleport out. Kill one and it may drop an **ender pearl**.

## Why you want pearls

Thrown ender pearls teleport you to where they land (at the cost of a little fall damage) — a niche [[Transportation]] trick on its own. More importantly, pearls combine with blaze powder from [[The Nether]] into **eyes of ender**, the item that locates strongholds and opens the portal to [[The End]]. Every playthrough that reaches the [[Ender Dragon]] goes through enderman pockets first. Later, piglin bartering and an End-side farm make pearls plentiful.

## Odd habits

Endermen quietly pick up certain blocks — grass, sand, TNT — and wander off with them, slowly landscaping your base one block at a time. They teleport away from rain as if personally offended by weather. And wearing a carved pumpkin on your head lets you look straight at them with no consequences, at the fashionable cost of seeing the world through a jack-o-lantern.
