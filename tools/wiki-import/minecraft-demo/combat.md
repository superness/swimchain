# Combat

**Combat** in [[Minecraft]] is short, scrappy, and mostly about preparation. The [[Mobs]] that want you dead are predictable; the players who die to them usually skipped something on this page.

## Weapons and timing

Attacks recharge: swing before the cooldown finishes and the hit lands weak. The **sword** is the standard — fast recovery, a sweep that hits crowds, and cheap to make at every tier ([[Tools and Armor]]). The **axe** hits harder per blow but recovers slowly — a duelist's weapon. Jumping and striking on the way down scores a **critical hit**. The **bow** delivers heavy damage at full draw, the **crossbow** trades rate of fire for punch and can be kept loaded, and the **trident** — dropped by drowned — throws, returns with Loyalty, and in the rain can fly you into the sky with Riptide.

## Defense

The **shield** blocks almost all frontal damage — arrows, blast waves, melee — and turns a [[Creeper]] detonation from a funeral into a loud noise. Armor reduces everything else. Keep your hunger bar full: above nine drumsticks you regenerate health steadily, which is why [[Farming]] is secretly a combat skill.

## Enemy habits worth knowing

Zombies crowd doorways and break wooden doors on Hard. Skeletons strafe and shoot — close the gap or duel around cover. Spiders climb walls and lose hostility in daylight. The [[Enderman]] only fights if provoked (or looked at), and the [[Creeper]] is silent right up until it isn't. In [[The Nether]], ghast fireballs can be batted back at the sender, and blazes die embarrassingly fast to thrown snowballs.

## Force multipliers

[[Enchanting]] adds Sharpness, Protection, and friends; [[Brewing]] supplies healing, strength, and fire resistance — the potion that makes lava merely inconvenient. Boss fights get their own dossiers: [[Ender Dragon]] and [[Wither]].
