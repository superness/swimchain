# Crafting

![Pixel-art crafting table]({{media:crafting.png}})

**Crafting** is how raw materials become everything else in [[Minecraft]]: tools, weapons, blocks, food, and machines. You start with the 2×2 grid in your inventory; a **crafting table** (four planks) upgrades you to the 3×3 grid that most recipes require.

## How recipes work

Recipes are either *shaped* — the arrangement in the grid matters — or *shapeless*, where only the ingredients matter. Shaped recipes are usually pictographic: a pickaxe is three of a material across the top with two sticks down the middle, a sword is two of a material stacked over a stick, a ladder looks like a ladder. Once you have seen a few, you can often guess the rest, and the in-game recipe book fills in anything you currently have materials for.

## Recipes worth memorizing

- **Sticks**: two planks, stacked. The other half of nearly every tool.
- **Torches**: coal over a stick. Light suppresses hostile [[Mobs]] spawns.
- **Furnace**: eight cobblestone in a ring — the door to [[Smelting]].
- **Bed**: three wool over three planks. Skips the night and sets your respawn.
- **Chest**: eight planks in a ring. You will always need more chests.
- **Shield**: six planks around an iron ingot — the cornerstone of [[Combat]].

## Stations beyond the table

Crafting branches into specialized blocks: the furnace family ([[Smelting]]), the brewing stand ([[Brewing]]), the enchanting table ([[Enchanting]]), the **anvil** for combining and repairing gear, the **smithing table** for the [[Diamond]]-to-[[Netherite]] upgrade, and the **stonecutter**, which cuts decorative stone with less waste than the equivalent grid recipes.

## Material flow

A typical chain: logs → planks → table and sticks; cobblestone → furnace; iron → bucket, shield, and armor ([[Tools and Armor]]); then down through [[Mining]] to diamonds. Keeping a chest of pre-crafted intermediates — planks, sticks, torches — turns every later project from a scavenger hunt into assembly.
