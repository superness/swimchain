# Farming

**Farming** is how you stop foraging and start living in [[Minecraft]]. Hunger fuels sprinting and healing, so food security is the quiet foundation under every other project on this wiki — you cannot brave [[The Nether]] on dry bread.

## Crops

Till grass or dirt next to water with a hoe and plant. The staples:

- **Wheat** — from seeds in tall grass; bakes into bread and breeds cows and sheep.
- **Carrots and potatoes** — found in [[Villager]] gardens and zombie drops; potatoes bake ([[Smelting]]) into one of the best early foods.
- **Beetroot** — soup material and pig feed.
- **Pumpkins and melons** — grow from a stalk onto adjacent ground; pumpkins also make golems and Halloween fashion.
- **Sugar cane** — grows on sand or dirt beside water; paper for books ([[Enchanting]] needs a lot of them) and sugar for [[Brewing]].
- **Nether wart** — the base of almost every potion; grows only on soul sand, imported from [[The Nether]].

Crops need light and grow in random ticks; **bone meal** (from skeleton bones or a composter) force-grows them instantly. Farmland trampled by jumping reverts to dirt — fence your fields against [[Mobs]] and your own carelessness.

## Animals

Feed two animals of a kind their favorite food and they produce a baby: wheat for cows and sheep, carrots or potatoes for pigs, seeds for chickens. Cows give beef, leather, and milk (which cures status effects); sheep give wool for beds; chickens lay eggs on their own schedule. A fenced pasture of each is a renewable supply of everything [[Crafting]] keeps asking for.

## Scaling up

Water hydrates farmland four blocks in every direction, so the classic layout is a 9×9 plot with one water block at center. Later, [[Redstone]] turns farming into infrastructure: observer-driven sugar cane harvesters, villager-powered crop farms, and automatic chicken cookers that feed directly into hoppers. The [[Villager]] farmer will even tend wheat for you and share the bread.
