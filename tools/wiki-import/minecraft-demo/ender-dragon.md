# Ender Dragon

The **Ender Dragon** is [[Minecraft]]'s final boss — a vast black dragon circling the central island of [[The End]], and the fight that rolls the credits when it dies. It is also entirely optional, which is very Minecraft.

## Getting to the fight

The road is the game's main quest in miniature: collect ender pearls from the [[Enderman]], blaze rods from a fortress in [[The Nether]], and craft eyes of ender. Thrown eyes fly toward the nearest **stronghold**; inside is a portal frame that activates when all twelve slots hold an eye. Step through and there is no way home except victory, death, or a long pearl-assisted escape — so arrive prepared: solid [[Tools and Armor]], a bow, food, blocks, and slow-falling potions from [[Brewing]] are all money well spent.

## The arena

The dragon circles ten obsidian pillars topped with **end crystals** — and the crystals are the actual first phase of the fight, because any crystal with line of sight heals the dragon faster than you can hurt it. Shoot them with a bow; the caged ones need climbing or careful long shots. Every crystal detonates violently when broken, which is also worth knowing for reasons the [[Wither]] page will make clear.

## The fight

With the crystals gone, the dragon alternates between strafing you with its breath, perching on the central bedrock fountain, and charging. While it perches, its head is briefly in reach — that is your melee window ([[Combat]] crit-timing applies). Its wing buffets fling you skyward, so a water bucket or slow falling is cheap life insurance. Beds famously explode when used in The End; famously, speedrunners use exactly that as a weapon.

## The spoils

Death drops a fountain of experience — enough to power serious [[Enchanting]] — plus a dragon egg trophy and an open exit portal. The outer End islands beyond hold shulkers, chorus fruit, and the elytra ([[Transportation]]'s endgame). The dragon can even be re-summoned with four new crystals, if you liked it.
