## Week 4 — We Hope You Disagree With Us

**LinkedIn**

Here's a strange thing to say about software: we hope you disagree with us.

Swimchain is an open-source protocol — thirteen published specifications covering every rule a node enforces. No company owns it. Which means no one, including the people who wrote it, gets the final word on what it becomes.

If a community decides the rules should be different, they can fork it. Take the code, change the rule, run their network their way. On a platform, that's betrayal. On a protocol, it's a feature. Forks are how a network stays honest: the current design has to keep earning its users, forever.

2027 is a horizon year — the Android path maturing, more machines lit up, more communities deciding what their corners look like. The honest truth is we don't fully know what Swimchain becomes next. That's not evasion. That's the design.

So: read the specs. Run a node. Argue with the rules. The network belongs to whoever shows up to shape it.
