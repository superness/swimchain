## Week 2 — Not for Most People

**LinkedIn**

It's not for you if you want an infinite feed picked by an algorithm. Your feed is built by your own node.

It IS for you if you're tired of being the product. If you want a network with no company, no servers, no ads — just people running nodes, keeping alive the things they care about, on purpose.
